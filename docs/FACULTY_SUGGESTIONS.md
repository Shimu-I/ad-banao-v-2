## Process

1. Add the suggestion to the table above on the day it is given, in the raw words used.
2. We decide: accept, change or decline, with a reason.
3. An accepted suggestion gets a goal ID in the [roadmap](07-roadmap.md), or changes an existing goal.
4. The change is recorded in `PROGRESS.md` history and in the changelog.
5. Nothing is silently dropped. A declined suggestion keeps its reason in the table.
